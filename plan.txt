BandhanMil — Multi-caste Matrimony Product Plan
================================================

Phase status
------------
[DONE] Phase 1 — Auth & Onboarding
  - Email/Mobile OTP login & registration
  - Multi-step onboarding: gender, identity, Religion→Caste→Sub-caste, career/location
  - Session-protected home stub

[NEXT] Phase 2 — Profiles & Search
  - Detailed profile management, photos/privacy
  - Smart search & multi-caste filters

[LATER] Phase 3 — Interests & Chat
  - Send Interest / Connect
  - Chat for paid/connected users

[LATER] Phase 4 — Admin & Monetization

Original feature notes
----------------------
A. User Panel (For Candidates & Families)
Registration & Onboarding: Email/Mobile OTP verification, Basic Details (Age, Caste, Religion, Education, Occupation, Income).

Multi-Caste & Religion Filters: Users apni caste, sub-caste, religion, location, aur mother tongue ke hisaab se filter kar sakein.

Detailed Profile Management: Photo uploads (with privacy settings), Horoscope/Kundali matching (optional integration), Family details.

Smart Search & Filter: Quick search, Advanced search, aur Keyword search.

Interactions:
"Send Interest" / "Connect" request.
Chat/Messaging (only for paid/connected users).
Shortlist / Favorite profiles.
Privacy Controls: Option to hide photo/phone number until interest is accepted.

B. Admin Panel (For You & Your Team)
User Management: Approve/Reject profiles, verify ID documents (Aadhaar/Passport).
Caste & Religion Master: Multi-caste support ke liye backend se naye Religions, Castes, aur Sub-castes add/manage karne ka system.
Subscription Management: Plans create karna (Free, Silver, Gold, Diamond).
Success Stories & Content: Reviews aur blogs manage karna.
Analytics Dashboard: Revenue, daily active users, aur conversion rates track karne ke liye.

C. Monetization Model
Freemium Model: Profile banana free, lekin Phone Number dekhna ya direct Chat karne ke liye Paid Membership.
Profile Highlighting: Premium profiles ko search results me sabse upar dikhane ke extra charges.
Assisted Matrimony: Personal Relationship Manager ki service dena (High-end clients ke liye).
